Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Amp is a personal, single-user tool that its owner uses to create slideshows and send them to their own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner connects TikTok, Amp receives the account's basic profile (display name, username, profile picture and account ID) and an access token that lets it send draft posts to that account's TikTok inbox.

How it is used and stored

This information is used only to send the owner's own slideshows to the owner's own account. It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Slide images

To let TikTok collect a slideshow's images, Amp temporarily publishes them at this website. They are removed automatically after about 14 days.

Your choices

The owner can disconnect TikTok in Amp at any time, and can revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.
